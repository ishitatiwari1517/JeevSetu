# PetVue — Customer Pain Point Research

> *Marketing campaigns are built around pain. Here's every problem PetVue solves, the solution it offers, and the outcome the user experiences.*

---

## The Core Insight

**Pets can't tell you when something is wrong.** By the time symptoms are obvious, conditions may have already progressed. This creates a cycle of anxiety, reactive care, and expensive emergency visits.

PetVue breaks this cycle by giving pet owners an AI-powered early detection tool they can use anytime, anywhere.

---

## Pain Point Matrix

### 1. "I don't know if my pet is sick"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | Pet owners notice something — a watery eye, a skin patch, unusual behavior — but can't tell if it's serious. Googling symptoms leads to fear-mongering forums and worst-case scenarios. |
| **Emotional State** | Anxiety, helplessness, "Am I overreacting?" |
| **Current Behavior** | Google → Reddit → panic → expensive vet visit (or ignore and hope) |
| **PetVue Solution** | AI health scan: upload a photo → get a structured assessment in seconds with severity grading and confidence scoring |
| **Outcome** | **Informed clarity** — know whether it's "totally normal" or "see a vet today" |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"Stop Googling. Start scanning."* |

---

### 2. "Vet visits are expensive and I can't afford unnecessary ones"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | Average vet visit costs ₹500–₹3,000 (India) / $50–$300 (US). Many visits end with "it's nothing, come back if it gets worse." Pet owners feel they wasted money — but were too scared not to go. |
| **Emotional State** | Frustration, financial stress, guilt ("I should go but can't afford it") |
| **Current Behavior** | Delay vet visits → conditions worsen → emergency visit costs 5x more |
| **PetVue Solution** | AI pre-screening that tells you *before* you go whether it's likely serious. If the scan says HEALTHY with high confidence, you can wait. If it says SEE_VET, you go with data. |
| **Outcome** | **Smart spending** — vet visits become intentional, not anxiety-driven |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"Know before you go. Save money. Save worry."* |

---

### 3. "I miss behavior changes until it's too late"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | Subtle signs — eating slightly less, sleeping more, a minor limp — are easy to miss or dismiss. These are often early indicators of treatable conditions. |
| **Emotional State** | Guilt, regret ("I should have noticed earlier") |
| **Current Behavior** | Only react when symptoms become undeniable |
| **PetVue Solution** | Regular AI scans create a health timeline. The History tab shows how things are changing over time. Scan today, compare next week. |
| **Outcome** | **Early detection** — catch it at "monitor" instead of "emergency" |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"The best time to check was yesterday. The next best time is now."* |

---

### 4. "I leave my pet alone and I worry about them"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | Working professionals, travelers, and busy families leave pets alone for hours. They worry: Is my pet okay? Are they eating? Is something wrong? |
| **Emotional State** | Guilt, separation anxiety (for the owner!) |
| **Current Behavior** | Pet cameras (passive observation), asking neighbors to check, worrying silently |
| **PetVue Solution** | Quick scan when you return home — takes 15 seconds. Builds a habit of regular health checks that catches changes early. |
| **Outcome** | **Peace of mind** — a daily 15-second health check becomes routine |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"Check in on what matters most — in 15 seconds."* |

---

### 5. "I don't know what's normal behavior for my pet's breed/age"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | First-time pet owners especially struggle to distinguish breed-typical characteristics from health concerns. "Is this discharge normal for bulldogs?" "Do all puppies sleep this much?" |
| **Emotional State** | Confusion, information overload |
| **Current Behavior** | Breed forums, YouTube, asking friends who also don't know |
| **PetVue Solution** | AI-generated reference points in scan results provide educational context. "This is within normal range for Golden Retrievers" vs. "This is atypical and worth monitoring." |
| **Outcome** | **Educated pet parenting** — learn what's normal as you scan |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"Your pet's breed manual — powered by AI."* |

---

### 6. "My vet visit is inefficient — I forget what to tell them"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | Pet owners arrive at the vet and can't clearly describe symptoms, timeline, or changes. This leads to longer appointments, repeat visits, and missed information. |
| **Emotional State** | Frustration, feeling unprepared, "I wish I'd written it down" |
| **Current Behavior** | Wing it at the vet; vague descriptions like "he's just been... off" |
| **PetVue Solution** | Pre-visit intake form captures symptoms, severity, duration, frequency, recent changes, and notes — structured and ready to share with the vet |
| **Outcome** | **Productive vet visits** — shorter, more focused, better outcomes |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"Walk into the vet like you've done your homework."* |

---

### 7. "I can't find a good vet nearby, especially in emergencies"

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Problem** | When the scan says URGENT, the pet owner needs a vet *now*. But finding a 24/7 clinic at 11pm, especially in a new city, is stressful. |
| **Emotional State** | Panic, urgency, helplessness |
| **Current Behavior** | Google Maps → call 3 clinics → get voicemail → drive 30 minutes to one that's open |
| **PetVue Solution** | Integrated vet finder with "Find 24/7 Vet Near Me" button directly on the scan result screen |
| **Outcome** | **Immediate action** — scan detects urgency → vet is one tap away |
| **Marketing Angle** | *"When seconds matter, PetVue knows where to go."* |
