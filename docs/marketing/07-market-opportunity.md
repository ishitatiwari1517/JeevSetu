## Pet Industry Growth Drivers

### 1. Pet Ownership Is Surging
- **India**: Pet population grew 20% during 2020–2024; expected to exceed 100 million pets by 2028 (Euromonitor)
- **US**: 66% of households own a pet (86.9 million homes) — all-time high (APPA 2024)
- **Global**: 1.5 billion pet-owning households worldwide

### 2. Pet Spending Is Rising Faster Than Pet Adoption
- US pet owners spent $136.8 billion in 2022, up from $123.6 billion in 2021 — **10.7% YoY growth** (APPA)
- In India, per-pet spending increased ~30% in 2 years, driven by premium food, health services, and grooming (IMARC)

### 3. Millennials & Gen Z Are the New Pet Parents
- 32% of Millennial pet owners and 26% of Gen Z pet owners use a pet health app (Packaged Facts, 2023)
- These demographics expect **digital-first, AI-powered experiences** — they're PetVue's core audience

### 4. The "Humanization" Trend
- 95% of pet owners consider their pet a family member (Harris Poll, 2023)
- Premium pet products (organic food, health supplements, tech devices) are the fastest-growing segment

### Key AI Pet Care Trends

1. **Computer Vision for Health** — AI-powered analysis of pet images and video is the fastest-growing AI pet tech segment, with projected 30%+ CAGR through 2030
2. **Predictive Health Analytics** — Using historical data + AI to predict health issues before symptoms appear
3. **Smart Feeding & Nutrition** — AI-personalized diet recommendations based on breed, age, weight, and activity
4. **Telehealth Integration** — AI triage before human vet consultations
5. **Wearable-Free Monitoring** — The shift from hardware (collars, trackers) to software-only (camera, photo) analysis — PetVue's exact approach

## India-Specific Market Opportunity

India is PetVue's initial target market, and it's a massive, underserved opportunity:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Pet dogs in India | 30+ million (and growing ~15% YoY) |
| Pet cats in India | 8+ million |
| Pet care spending (India, 2024) | ₹25,000+ crore (~$3.5B) |
| Organized vet market penetration | <15% (huge gap for tech) |
| Smartphone users in India | 750+ million |
| Pet-focused app adoption | <5% of pet owners |

**The white space**: India has massive pet ownership, growing spending, smartphone ubiquity — but virtually no AI-powered pet health tools. PetVue can define this category.
