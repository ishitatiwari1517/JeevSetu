> *This is what your entire GTM strategy depends on. Know your customer better than they know themselves.*

## Primary Persona: The Pet Parent

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | "Priya" / "Alex" |
| **Age** | 25–45 |
| **Income** | ₹6L–25L / $40K–$120K annually |
| **Geography** | Urban/suburban India (Tier 1 & 2 cities), USA, UK |
| **Pet Type** | Dogs (primary), cats (secondary) |
| **Tech Savviness** | Moderate to high — comfortable with smartphones, apps, and AI |
| **Education** | College-educated |
| **Household** | Single professionals, young couples, families with children |

### Psychographics

- Treats their pet like a family member (often refers to themselves as a "pet parent")
- Active on social media — shares pet photos regularly
- Willing to spend on premium pet products but wants value justification
- Anxious about their pet's health, especially when leaving them alone
- Prefers self-service digital tools over phone calls

### Pain Points

1. **"I don't know if this is normal"** — Sees something on their pet and can't tell if it's serious
2. **"Vet visits are expensive"** — Wants to avoid unnecessary visits but fears missing real issues
3. **"I forget their health history"** — No centralized record of scans, vaccinations, or vet visits
4. **"I feel guilty leaving them alone"** — Worries about their pet when at work or traveling
5. **"I don't know what's changed"** — Subtle behavioral shifts go unnoticed until they escalate

### Motivations

- Peace of mind about their pet's health
- Feeling like a responsible, informed pet parent
- Convenience — everything in one app
- Early detection to prevent bigger (and costlier) problems
- Social validation — sharing their pet's health score

### How PetVue Serves Them

| Pain Point | PetVue Solution |
|-----------|----------------|
| "Is this normal?" | AI scan with plain-language explanation |
| "Vet visits are expensive" | Pre-screening to avoid unnecessary visits |
| "I forget health history" | Scan history + pet profile |
| "I don't know what to do" | Precautionary steps + "when to see vet" guidance |
| "I need a vet now" | Integrated vet finder + booking |

### Buying Behavior

- **Discovery**: Social media (Instagram, YouTube), Google search, pet communities, word-of-mouth
- **Decision Trigger**: A health scare with their pet OR seeing a friend use the app
- **Conversion Path**: Free AI scan → value realization → subscription for unlimited scans + features
- **Retention Driver**: Recurring scans, scan history, reminders, and integrated booking

## Secondary Persona 1: Veterinarians

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | "Dr. Sharma" / "Dr. Chen" |
| **Age** | 30–55 |
| **Practice** | Solo practice or small-medium clinic |
| **Pain Points** | Overwhelmed with basic consultations; clients arrive unprepared; missed early-stage conditions |

### Why They'd Care About PetVue

- **Intake Form Integration**: Pre-filled symptom data saves 5–10 minutes per appointment
- **Triage Assistance**: AI pre-screening helps prioritize urgent vs. routine cases
- **Client Education**: Pet owners arrive informed rather than panicked
- **Referral Revenue**: Vet partner program — PetVue sends booking traffic

### Engagement Model

- B2B partnership (clinic onboarding)
- White-label AI scan for clinic-branded use
- Referral commissions on bookings

### Profile

## Secondary Persona 3: Pet Insurance Companies

### Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | "PetSure" / "PawProtect" |
| **Interest** | Reducing claims through preventive care |

### Why They'd Care About PetVue

- **Preventive Care Incentives**: Insured pet owners who scan regularly catch conditions earlier → lower claim costs
- **Risk Data**: Aggregate (anonymized) scan data helps price policies more accurately
- **Policy Bundling**: Offer PetVue Premium as a policy perk

### Engagement Model

- API partnership for scan data integration
- Co-branded wellness programs
- Bundle PetVue subscription with insurance plans
